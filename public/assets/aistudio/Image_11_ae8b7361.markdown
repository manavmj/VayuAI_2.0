## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Organic Minimalism** with **Civic Precision**. It pairs the clarity and trust of an institutional environmental monitoring interface with the invigorating, breathable energy of nature. Designed for municipal leaders, public health analysts, climate activists, and everyday citizens, the system evokes vitality, crystalline freshness, clarity, and uncompromising institutional reliability.

The visual style is defined by luminous white negative space, fluid natural silhouettes, crisp micro-borders, and rich botanical depths. Instead of sterile corporate tech or alarmist climate readouts, the interface communicates proactive renewal and clear civic action through airy elevation, verdant color gradients, and human-centered data presentation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, clean-air ambiance anchored by institutional botanical greens:

- **Primary (`#15803d` / Forest Deep):** The core brand color, anchoring primary interactive buttons, prominent civic verification marks, and key air quality status thresholds.
- **Secondary (`#16a34a` / Fresh Emerald):** Drives supportive navigation, secondary actions, active toggles, and live sensor stream indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#eab308` / Sunny Flora):** Used purposefully as an accent for alert highlights, moderate air quality indexes, sensor calibration pings, and sunny micro-interactions.
- **Neutral (`#0f172a` / Deep Slate):** High-contrast, sharp typography color providing legibility against crisp backgrounds.
- **Surface Foundations:** Grounded in pure `#ffffff` and ambient `#f8fafc`, accented with pale botanical washes (`#f0fdf4` and `#dcfce7`) to mimic the diffusion of light through canopy foliage.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Outfit** for headlines and display units with **Inter** for data grids, body copy, and civic metrics. 

- **Outfit** carries an aerodynamic geometry with open circular counters, conveying forward-thinking public health intelligence and optimism.
- **Inter** ensures high legibility across dense environmental data streams, sensor feeds, tabular readouts, and map legends.
- Use uppercase styling strictly for `label-sm` and categorical badge tags, pairing them with relaxed letter spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain an open, non-punitive tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable **12-column fluid grid** desktop paradigm scaling down to a **4-column structure on mobile devices**. 

- **Canvas Inset & Breathing Room:** Generous outer margins ensure the design feels open and unconstrained. Content cards float within expansive whitespace to avoid data overcrowding.
- **Asymmetric Canopy Framing:** Secondary hero regions and dashboard summaries can incorporate gentle, large-radius asymmetrical corner treatments (e.g., top-right curvilinear masks or soft organic cutaways) mimicking topographic terraces and tree canopies.
- **Density Controls:** Analytical dashboards compress component spacing to `space-sm` and `space-md` for high data throughput, while civic landing views and public health alerts expand out to `space-lg` and `space-xl` for maximum readability.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is conveyed through **tonal glassmorphism** and **ambient botanical shadows**:

- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** `#ffffff` or muted daylight canvas `#f8fafc`.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Solid `#ffffff` or translucent glass `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Enclosed by an ultra-subtle border: `1px solid rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Controls & Popovers):** Supported by diffused, leaf-tinted shadows rather than harsh neutral blacks. Shadows use an expanded blur radius with low opacity: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Air Alerts):** Raised using multi-tiered ambient diffusion (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.16)`) and anchored over a daylight tinted backdrop overlay `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The shape structure uses standard rounded geometries (`roundedness: 2`, matching `0.5rem` base radii) for controls, inputs, and modular cards, transitioning smoothly to **pill silhouettes (`9999px`)** for chips, civic verification badges, and primary search bars.

Hero imagery and primary dashboard breakout banners employ organic asymmetric curvature (e.g., custom border radii such as `3rem 1rem 3rem 1rem`) to subtly echo natural contours, river basins, and wind currents without sacrificing interface predictability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid gradient or fill using `#15803d` transitioning subtly to `#16a34a`. High-contrast white typography (`#ffffff`), rounded corners (`0.5rem` or full pill for call-to-actions), and an ambient green glow on hover (`0 8px 20px -2px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#f0fdf4`, border `1px solid rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.25)`, text `#15803d`. Hover changes surface to `#dcfce7`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#15803d` text; used strictly for low-priority links and tertiary actions.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Civic Trust Badges:** Full pill shape, background `#f0fdf4`, border `1px solid #bbf7d0`, text `#14532d`, accompanied by an emerald shield or verified checkmark icon.
- **Air Quality Status Chips:** Dynamically colored based on sensor indexes (Good: `#dcfce7` text `#15803d`; Moderate: `#fef9c3` text `#854d0e`), fixed with a glowing pulse dot.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Inputs:** Pill-shaped, semi-translucent surface (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.9)`), framed in `1px solid #e2e8f0`. Focus switches the outline to a crisp `2px solid #16a34a` with zero chromatic bleed.
- **Standard Forms:** Clean rectangular inputs with `0.5rem` radius, light slate placeholders (`#94a3b8`), and clear label anchors set in `label-md`.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Air Metric Cards:** Crisp `#ffffff` surface, soft `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` outer stroke, inner padding of `space-lg`. Metrics feature bold Outfit numerical values paired with Inter tracking labels.
- **Overlay Panels:** Translucent white panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) suspended over geographic and sensor map layers, utilizing `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circle controls. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #cbd5e1` on clean white. Checked: `#16a34a` fill with a crisp white tick mark and a soft outward ring.
